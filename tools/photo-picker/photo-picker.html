MVP Media Photo Picker

Runs entirely in this browser window. Your photos are never uploaded anywhere; the tool only saves files to your Downloads folder.

1Make contact sheets

  1. Choose the folder that holds your photos. Subfolders are included, and each subfolder (shoot) gets its own sheets.
  2. Press Make contact sheets. Every photo gets a number printed under its thumbnail.
  3. Zip files land in your Downloads folder, each under 24 MB. Send them to Claude one at a time.

2Export and auto-edit the picks

  1. Choose the same folder as in step 1, so the numbers match.
  2. Paste the numbers Claude picked. Commas, spaces, new lines, and ranges like 120-135 all work.
  3. Press Export picks. Each photo is automatically edited (color cast removed, brightness and contrast balanced, lightly sharpened), cropped if you choose a shape, resized, and saved as a web-ready JPEG with location and camera data removed, in zips under 24 MB.

Works with JPEG, PNG, and WebP in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari. iPhone HEIC photos work in Safari on a Mac; in other browsers, export them as JPEG first. Camera RAW files (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG) are skipped: export JPEGs from your editing app. If the browser asks to allow multiple downloads, choose Allow.
